Our Story

A Bold Idea For A Greener Tomorrow

What started as a simple question in a college dorm turned into Greenomers — a journey of determination, collaboration and innovation to fight plastic pollution.

2023

A 19-Year-Old With a Bigger Question.

At just 19, Jeet Lukhi realized how deeply plastic was harming our planet. That question became the beginning of Greenomers.

2024

Three Minds. One Vision.

Jeet met Raj and Rahul. Together they decided to team up and turn the idea into reality.

2025

Ideas Into Real Solutions.

The team established its first unit, experimented with materials and formulations, and tested real-world solutions.

2026

From Possibility to Production.

After successful testing and validation, Greenomers moved toward scalable commercial production.
