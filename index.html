Our Story

A Bold Idea For A Greener Tomorrow

What started as a simple question in a college dorm turned into Greenomers — a journey of determination, collaboration and innovation to fight plastic pollution.

2023

A 19-Year-Old With a Bigger Question.

At just 19, Jeet Lukhi realized how deeply plastic was harming our planet. What began as a question became the beginning of Greenomers.

2024

Three Minds. One Vision.

Jeet met Raj and Rahul, bringing together strategy, execution and technical expertise around one shared vision — a cleaner planet.

2025

Ideas Into Real Solutions.

The team established its first unit, experimenting with materials and formulations to develop practical packaging solutions.

2026

From Possibility to Production.

After successful testing and validation, Greenomers moved toward commercial production with scalable and reliable manufacturing.

Our Granules

Engineered for Diverse Applications.

Our high-performance compostable granules are designed to replace conventional plastics across industries, offering a sustainable and cost-effective alternative.

01

Blown Film Granules

Designed to replace traditional plastic blown films for carry bags, garbage bags, packaging films and shrink films.

02

Extrusion Granules

Suitable for manufacturing straws and other extrusion products with reliable strength and smooth processing.

03

Injection Molding Granules

Developed for manufacturing cutlery, food containers, lids, caps, toys and injection molded products.

04

Injection Blow Molding Granules

Engineered for bottles and containers, providing durability, strength and sustainable performance.

05

Extrusion Blow Molding Granules

Suitable for larger hollow products such as detergent bottles, chemical containers, industrial drums and packaging.

06

Thermoforming Granules

Engineered for thermoforming applications such as meat trays, food trays, cups, plates and clamshells.
